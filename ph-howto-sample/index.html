How to cash in GCash at 7-Eleven

A clear, local how-to for people who need cash in their GCash wallet today — no bank app required. (Placeholder content for scaffolding.)

Show me the steps About this site

What you need

  • A verified GCash account on your phone
  • Cash (bills) and a nearby 7-Eleven
  • Your GCash mobile number ready to show at the counter

Steps

  1. Open GCash → Cash In
    Choose Partner outlets / 7-Eleven (labels may vary by app version).
  2. Bring cash to any 7-Eleven
    Tell the cashier you want to cash in to GCash. Show your registered number.
  3. Pay the amount + small fee
    Fees change; confirm on-screen before you hand over cash.
  4. Check your GCash balance
    You should see the cash-in appear within minutes. Keep the receipt.

FAQ

Is there a minimum cash-in?

Often a small minimum applies at partner outlets. Check the GCash app for the current amount.

Can someone else cash in for me?

Policies differ by outlet. Safest: do it yourself with your own phone and ID if asked.

What if the cash-in doesn’t show up?

Keep the receipt, note the time, and contact GCash support from the app. Do not send money to strangers offering “fix.”
